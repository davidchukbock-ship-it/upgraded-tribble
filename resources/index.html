Resources

Resources

Guides, calculators and tools for employers who are new to international hiring.

Knowledge

  • Global Hiring Glossary
  • Blog

    The blog will carry practical articles for employers new to international hiring: how permits work, what to ask a provider, how to plan a season.

  • Provider Reviews

    Choosing a staffing provider is hard when you cannot see their work.

HR tools

  • Salary Calculator

    The salary calculator is a tool that shows typical pay for a role in a given country.

  • Onboarding Calculator

    The onboarding calculator is a tool that estimates the time and cost of bringing a new worker on board.

  • Entity Calculator

    The entity calculator is a tool that compares the cost of opening your own company abroad with other options.

  • Severance Calculator

    The severance calculator is a tool that estimates what an employer may owe when a job ends, by country.

  • Misclassification Check

    The misclassification check is a tool that helps you see whether someone is really a contractor or should be an employee.

Finance tools

  • Global Payroll Calculator

    The global payroll calculator is a tool that turns a gross salary into the real employer cost in a given country.

  • Paystub Generator

    The paystub generator is a tool that creates a clear pay statement from the numbers you enter.

  • 1099 Generator

    The 1099 generator is a tool that prepares the US form 1099 that reports payments to contractors.

  • W-2 Generator

    The W-2 generator is a tool that prepares the US form W-2 that reports an employee’s yearly pay and taxes.

Country guides

  • Country Guides

    A country guide explains, for one country, the basics an employer must know: types of employment, notice periods, taxes, public holidays and the permits workers need.
