Employ

Employ

Employment models, payroll and the practical side of having a team abroad.

Hire anywhere

  • Employer of Record

    An employer of record, or EOR, is a licensed local company that legally employs your worker.

  • Contractor of Record

    A contractor of record manages the paperwork and payment for independent contractors: the agreement, invoices, tax rules and the risk of misclassification.

  • Contractor Payments

    Contractor payments is a service that pays your contractors in their own country and currency, on time, and keeps a record of each payment.

  • US PEO

    A PEO, professional employer organization, is a US company that shares employer duties with you: payroll, benefits, workers’ compensation and compliance.

  • How It Works

Pay & protect your team

  • Global Payroll

    Global payroll means paying employees in several countries correctly: salary, taxes, social contributions and payslips, each following local rules.

  • Global Benefits

    Global benefits are the extras that come with a job: health insurance, pension, paid leave.

Relocate talent

  • Visa & Immigration

    Visa and immigration covers the permits a person needs to live and work in another country.

Simplify operations

  • Background Checks

    A background check confirms basic facts about a candidate: identity, work history, education and, where the law allows, criminal record.

  • IT Equipment

    IT equipment service means a laptop, phone and accessories arrive at the worker’s door in another country, set up and ready, and are collected when the job ends.

  • Office & Car Leasing

    Leasing means renting an office space or a vehicle for a set period instead of buying.

  • Command Center

    A command center is one place, and one contact person, where you see your whole international team: who works where, which documents are due and what needs your decision.
