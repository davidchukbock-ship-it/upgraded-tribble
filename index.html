Advisers to the EBRD

GCN Workforce works as an adviser to the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD), an international financial institution that supports private-sector development.

What this means for you

  • Professional standards. Advisory work for an international financial institution calls for transparent processes and a high level of professional conduct.
  • Knowledge of European markets. We understand labor markets, business conditions and regulation across the EU, and use that experience for our clients.
  • Confidence for partners and employers. You work with a team that has experience in projects of international scale.

Our role as an adviser does not mean that the EBRD endorses or guarantees GCN Workforce’s commercial services.

FAQ

Questions employers ask

Which markets do you serve?

Current service across the EU; Canada through authorized partners; the United States is planned through GCN Workforce LLC (Texas).

Do you guarantee visas or work authorization?

No. Government decisions are outside our control. We plan every project around the legal requirements of each country.

What is the difference between staffing and outstaffing?

Staffing: we find and prepare people and you hire them directly. Outstaffing: our team works in your business, employed by us as the licensed employer where local law allows, supervised by our senior team manager and accepted through acts of completed work.

How quickly will I get an answer?

Usually within one business day.

Can I become a partner or look for work?

Yes. Partners join through our Telegram bot; candidates can use the “Looking for work?” link in the footer.
