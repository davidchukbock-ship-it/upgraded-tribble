Recruit

Recruitment services and the industries and regions we plan to cover.

Our solutions

  • International Recruitment

    International recruitment means finding candidates in other countries for a job in yours.

  • Recruitment with Employer of Record

    Here the candidates are found by the recruiter and then employed by an employer of record, a licensed company that signs the contracts and runs payroll.

  • Executive Search

    Executive search is a targeted hunt for senior managers and rare specialists.

  • RPO (Recruitment Process Outsourcing)

    RPO, recruitment process outsourcing, means a provider runs part or all of your hiring process for you, from job ads to offers, as an extension of your HR team.

  • Embedded Recruiters

    An embedded recruiter is a specialist who works as part of your team for a set period, using your tools and your employer brand, while being employed by the provider.

  • Contract & Temp Staffing

    Contract and temporary staffing provides workers for a fixed period or a specific task.

  • Talent Mapping

    Talent mapping is research into where the people you need work today: which countries, which companies, how many, and at what pay level.

  • Employer Branding

    Employer branding is how candidates see you as a place to work.

Recruit by region

  • Asia-Pacific

    Asia-Pacific has large pools of skilled and semi-skilled workers in many countries.

  • Latin America

    Latin America offers Spanish and Portuguese speakers in compatible time zones for North American employers.

  • Europe & UK

    Europe is our best-known region: we have a proven model in Ukraine and work with partners in Central and Eastern Europe and the Baltics.

  • Middle East & Africa

    The Middle East and Africa combine young, growing labor markets with very different legal systems and travel rules.

Assess & vet

  • Personality Testing

    Personality tests describe how a person usually behaves at work: teamwork, stress, attention to detail.

  • Skills Assessment

    A skills assessment checks whether a person can do the work: a practical test, a short task or a demonstration on the job.

  • Cognitive Testing

    Cognitive tests measure how quickly a person learns, reasons and solves new problems, independent of past experience.

  • Reference Checking

    Reference checking means contacting former employers to confirm the dates, the role and the quality of a candidate’s work.

  • Interview-as-a-Service

    Interview-as-a-service means trained interviewers run structured interviews on your behalf and send you a written summary for each candidate.
