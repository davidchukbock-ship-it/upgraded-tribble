Connecting markets. Building opportunities.

GCN Workforce helps employers in the United States and Canada build reliable teams through international talent markets and a trusted partner network.

Who we are

GCN Workforce operates through GCN Workforce LLC in Texas, USA, with active operations and a partner network across the EU.

We combine our own capabilities with licensed partners and always state clearly which services are active, delivered through partners, or still in development.
